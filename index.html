Our Story

Inspired by the quiet side-streets of Tokyo, Kissa is a sanctuary from the bustling world. We believe in the beauty of slowness, pouring each cup with deliberate care and intention.

Our space is designed to feel like a warm embrace, where the aroma of freshly ground beans mingles with the soft hum of quiet conversations.

Whether you're seeking a solitary moment of reflection or a cozy gathering with friends, Kissa offers a gentle pause in your day.
